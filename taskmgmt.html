Job Order Application

A direct and simple job order application for a client that handles plumbing and sanitation services. Requires a split use for its service providers, clients and admins.

Problem: The clients needed a job order system that would track the maintenance progress for their technicians on the go. For the technicians on the ground, they specifically needed it on a mobile device — a tablet, which the client company would provide for carry around as they went about their jobs.

Designed and worked closely with a developer in creating a simple job order and task management application that would be managed between the client's service providers and staff. Worked to make the app simple and intuitive for laypeople to use as the technicians may not be familiar with digital mediums.

Our proposed solution: An application that would be able to clean areas routinely while working hand in hand with the rest of EF Software's existing products. Harnessing cleaning robotics and possibly the public as users of the product, we sought to have a system where the cleaning robot can routinely clean and activate whenever needed, alleviating some of the menial labour whereby a housekeeper would routinely have to do.

Key information that was relevant to the plumbers were identified to be placed into the dashboards, and items needed to be legible, synonymous in design yet standing out from one another — particularly with varying dashboards display different things (a short status summary vs job orders and their statuses). In designing the system a key aspect required understanding the process of a job order, breaking down the information and rearranging it so that the design will not be too overwhelming or cluttered.
