Floor Cleaning Kiosk System (with Robotics) for
SGH EM NNI

Timeline: 6 weeks (Research & Design, Rough testing with robotics, Development). Manpower can be assigned to higher-impact tasks.

Problem: The constant shift and lack of manpower in the housekeeping industry affects cleanliness within the facility. The client wanted to tackle the issue of reducing Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) hours, redistributing workload such that staff could focus on other more important tasks.

A housekeeper's standard tasks were identified, with routine area cleaning being the highest priority in which time spent should re-purposed to do better, impactful tasks.

Our proposed solution: An application that would be able to clean areas routinely while working hand in hand with the rest of EF Software's existing products. Harnessing cleaning robotics and possibly the public as users of the product, we sought to have a system where the cleaning robot can routinely clean and activate whenever needed, alleviating some of the menial labour whereby a housekeeper would routinely have to do.

In proposing the ideas, certain considerations were included:
- The tight timeline lead us towards a 2D map, as we were only working with a single floor in the building and had lesser need for a 3D map that would also consume more screen space.
- Would be good if the robot is able to clean specific locations.
- As the area proposed for this project is large, the product needs to be user friendly.

After initial prototyping, user feedback was also given:
- Larger landmarks to better define specific areas, helping the user identify where they are and where the spill may be located on the map.
- Further breakdown of larger zones so that the robot does not need to take the time to clean a large area with a small spill.
- Product is accessible to all, from the public aunty to the housekeeping supervisor. Hence it needs to be user-friendly, both the general consumer and a digital native.

Redesigned Smart Feedback System

User Feedback tablet, web & app design. Administrative SAAS design. Manpower can be assigned to higher-impact tasks.

The eSmart feedback and toilet system is a comprehensive feedback and management solution. that tracks the condition of a location by capturing feedback from IoT sensors and public feedback. User feedback and IoT captured feedback (such as traffic) is then collated and analysed to draw new insights on the area, allowing for improved manpower deployment and cleaner environments.

Project Overview: As the whole system was getting an overhaul in design, an update of new screens and smileys for the existing feedback systems were in order. New animations, feedback screens, and a fresh transition from legacy to SAAS mobile and web systems were required.

These feedback systems not only serve as a means for public users to rate the condition of the area, but to also provide feedback and report any faults found. The tablet also serves as a touchpoint for cleaners to digitally fill in their toilet cards as they clean each aspect of the environment.

Competitive Analysis : Unabiz, Convergent Smart Tech

The screen was revamped to also include QR codes, in the case of public not wanting to touch the tablet surface, they can then scan and be linked to a web portal to give their feedback. As the tablets were usually installed in a toilet, a QR code was also added so that users need not directly touch the tablet to give their feedback.

Further expansion of the design was also done to the overall eSmart Feedback system, including dashboards for management, the public, and also integration to other system solutions that are in EF Software's superapp.
