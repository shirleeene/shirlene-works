Bespoke Jewellery Site Redesign

Design System UI Web Design B2C

Website Redesign

A website redesign that aligns with that of a bespoke jewellery store. Requires a split use for its service providers, clients and admins.

Problem: The client initially had two different websites already made — one acting as a store but heavy-laden with text and customer education (akin to a blog on diamonds), the other acting as a site appointment bookings. Their main site had a dated design and had no shop page element to it. It lacked the look and feel that bespoke jewellery had.

We sought to make a website that was clean and elegant while still carrying out their branding of colours and fonts. Some intentional design changes included:
- Focusing on their darker theme colour that would match their Instagram look and feel, which was the main communicating point between themselves and their audience.
- Looked at other competitors' shop pages and settled on a shop system that helped customers make step by step choices better suited for their engagement rings.
